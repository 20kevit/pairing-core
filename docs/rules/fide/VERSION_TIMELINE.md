## 2026-02-01 — 2026 Swiss Rules in force (CURRENT)

- Documents: all "effective from 1 February 2026" chapters above.
- Structural landmarks verified:
  - C.04.1: 9 numbered general rules replace lettered a–g [F-0101 supersedes F-0102].
  - C.04.2: rewritten handling rules [F-0103 supersedes F-0104].
  - C.04.3: [C1]–[C21] criteria optimisation replaces A–E search; PPB/CLB special
    brackets removed (2024, per TEC tutorial S-0106); PAB to lowest score.
  - C.04.4.1–3: Dubov/Burstein/Lim in force in 2026 formulations.
  - C.04.5: new standalone Double Swiss (pre-2026 C.04.5 was Accelerated).
  - C.04.6: Team Swiss current text [F-0115].
  - C.04.7: Baku Acceleration generalised beyond 1-½-0 scoring.
  - PAB default value: **win** unless regulations state otherwise (2026 C.04.1),
    vs draw pre-2026 — manager-side valuation, pairing-core input-model concern.
- Applicability re-confirmed by FIDE reminder [F-0301].
