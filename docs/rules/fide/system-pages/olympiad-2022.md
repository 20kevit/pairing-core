**Inspected entry points:** [`src/pairing_core/fide2026/olympiad.py::pair_olympiad`](../../../../src/pairing_core/fide2026/olympiad.py#L207)

- Source currentness is unknown: the 2022 chapter and effective date were retrieved, but the search is bounded and does not prove no later Olympiad rules exist.
