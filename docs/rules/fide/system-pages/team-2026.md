# Swiss Team Pairing System, 2026 edition

**System ID:** `team-2026`<br>
**System status:** `implemented_with_open_limitations`<br>
**Sources:** [F-0101](../source-pages/F-0101.md), [F-0103](../source-pages/F-0103.md), [F-0115](../source-pages/F-0115.md)

## Scope and limitations

Teams are pairing units. Team composition, lineups, match/game point valuation, primary/secondary score policy, team TPNs and roster management remain caller-owned.

**Inspected entry points:** [`src/pairing_core/fide2026/double_team.py::pair_double_or_team`](../../../../src/pairing_core/fide2026/double_team.py#L341)

Limitations:
- Type A/B/none colour preferences are represented, but complete per-clause independent verification remains open.
- Next-bracket look-ahead and search ordering are implementation strategies, not independent proof of full rule equivalence.
